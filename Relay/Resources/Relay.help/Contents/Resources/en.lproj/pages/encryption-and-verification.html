Privacy & Security

Encryption and session verification

About end-to-end encryption

Relay uses Matrix's end-to-end encryption (E2EE) to secure messages in encrypted rooms. Only participants in the room can read the messages. Encryption is enabled per-room when the room is created.

Session verification

When you sign in on a new device, you need to verify your session to access encrypted message history. A verification banner appears at the bottom of the sidebar until your session is verified.

Verify with another device

If you have another device already signed in:

  1. Click the verification banner, or go to Settings > Sessions and click Verify Session.
  2. Accept the verification request on your other device.
  3. Compare the 7 emoji shown on both devices.
  4. If they match, confirm on both devices.

Verify with a recovery key

If you don't have another device available:

  1. Click Use Recovery Key in the verification sheet.
  2. Enter your security/recovery key.
  3. Your session is verified and encrypted history becomes accessible.

Only your own verified sessions can receive your encryption keys from another session. If a session you have not verified asks for them, your other sessions do not answer.

Key backup

Relay can back up the keys that decrypt your messages to your homeserver. The backup is encrypted so that only you can read it. If you sign in on a new device, or lose one, the backup lets you read your message history again.

Go to Settings > Encryption to see the state of your key backup:

  • Key Backup Active: Relay is adding message keys to your backup. It shows how many keys are backed up and how many are waiting to upload.
  • Key Backup Not in Use: your account has a backup, but Relay is not adding keys to it. Verify this session first. If it is already verified, the backup was not signed by a device or key you trust.
  • Key Backup Not Active: your account has no backup.
  • Key Backup Unavailable: Relay could not check. Check your connection and try again.

Back up now

Relay uploads new message keys shortly after they arrive. To upload the keys that are waiting right away, click Back Up Now. The button appears when keys are waiting.

Set up a key backup

If your account has no backup, click Set Up Key Backup. Relay creates the backup and shows your recovery key once. Copy it and keep it somewhere safe, then confirm that you saved it. If your account had no secret storage, Relay creates it too, so the same recovery key also unlocks your message keys and identity in other apps, such as Element.

Keys already on this device join the backup as Relay reads your message history, so older messages may not be covered at first. If your account already has a backup, Relay never replaces it.

Restore from a key backup

Click Restore Key Backup to restore your message history. Relay uses the backup key stored on this device. If there is none, it asks your other sessions to share it. To restore with your recovery key instead, verify your session with it.

Messages that cannot be decrypted

A message that cannot be read shows Unable to decrypt with a reason:

  • Missing room key or Key does not cover this message: this session does not have the key for the message. Click Restore from Key Backup on the message. Relay also asks the sender's devices for the key automatically, and the message appears once a key arrives.
  • Duplicate encrypted message: the same encrypted message appeared again in a different event. Relay does not show it.
  • Megolm decryption failed, Malformed encrypted message, or Invalid decrypted content: the message was damaged, or it did not come from the device that created its key. Restoring a backup does not fix these.

Click Copy Session ID when you report a problem, so the developers can look up the key.

Encryption status

The Security tab in the room inspector shows whether encryption is enabled for a room. Once enabled, encryption cannot be turned off.

View session info

Go to Settings > Sessions to see all your signed-in devices, including device name, ID, last seen time, and IP address. Settings > Encryption shows your session's verification, key backup, and account recovery status.

Warning: Keep your recovery key in a safe place. If you lose access to all your devices and your recovery key, you will not be able to read your encrypted message history.
